FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this work?

Booking direct through this site saves you the service fee that Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com add on top of your stay. Once you book, we'll be in touch right away to confirm the reservation and next steps. Three days before check in, you'll receive the cabin's address and door code by email, no keys and no in person check in meeting required.

Do you have reviews?

This site doesn't host reviews directly, but both cabins are the same ones listed on the platforms, reviews and all. Bearadise: Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com. Rustic Retreat: Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com.

How do I check in?

Three days before arrival, you'll get a detailed email with cabin instructions and your access code. Both cabins use electronic locks, so you can drive straight to the cabin and check yourself in, no meeting a host required.

Are the cabins pet friendly?

While we love pets, we regret that the cabins are not pet friendly. The land around both is steep and doesn't give pets a good area to relieve themselves.

Is smoking allowed?

No, smoking isn't allowed inside or anywhere on the property at either cabin.

What's the minimum age to book?

The guest making the reservation must be at least 18. A couple of house rules go further: at Bearadise, everyone in the group must be 25 or older, with children accompanied by a parent welcome, and at both cabins, hot tub use is limited to guests 25 and up.

What's provided?

Both cabins come stocked with kitchen basics (cookware, dishes, and utensils), bath towels, high speed WiFi, and plenty of board games. Bearadise adds a private hot tub, a primary suite jacuzzi, and a full game room with a pool table. Rustic Retreat adds a private hot tub and a loft office nook. Each cabin's page has the full list.

How do I contact you once I've booked?

Once you've booked, you'll get access to a guest portal where you can message us with anything you need throughout your stay. Our contact form is only for questions before you book.

Where do I park?

Parking is a little different at each cabin. Bearadise has a driveway right in front of the porch with room for 5 to 6 cars parked perpendicular, or about 3 parked parallel, plus extra gravel parking nearby. At Rustic Retreat, you'll pass one driveway then a smaller one that leads right to the cabin, with plenty of room to park along either. Full details are on each cabin's page.

What's your cancellation policy?

Our cancellation and refund policy is being finalized and will be posted on our Cancellation Policy page and on each cabin's page. In the meantime, feel free to ask us directly before you book.
