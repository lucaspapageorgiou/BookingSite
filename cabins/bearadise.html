Good to Know

House Rules

  • 8 guests maximum, all guests must be over 25 (children with parents are welcome)
  • Check in 4:00 PM, check out 11:00 AM, no early check in or late checkout
  • No pets
  • No smoking
  • No parties, quiet hours start at 10 PM
  • No fires inside or outside the cabin
  • No alcohol or drugs at or near the hot tub
  • Please don't move the furniture
  • Start the dishwasher before checking out
  • No refunds for events outside our control, such as weather or power outages
  • There's wildlife around the property, you may spot different animals, please respect their home

Parking

There's a driveway right in front of the porch. Park perpendicular (head first) for 5 to 6 cars, or parallel to the cabin for about 3. Extra space is also available on the gravel near the picnic table.

  • All car types can access the cabin, a standard sedan has no trouble
  • The driveway is slightly steep, if there's snow on the ground, chains or all wheel drive are encouraged
  • There's a blind curve and a blind hill along the way, stay alert and you'll have nothing to worry about
  • The roads are fully paved
  • Roads are salted ahead of winter storms, but chains or all wheel drive are still encouraged if snow is on the ground
