Our rustic cabin is a true mountain escape with a distinctly secluded feel, though it's only a few miles from Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, and Dollywood. With open spaces throughout, it's the perfect destination for your visit to the Great Smoky Mountains.

You'll enjoy the open concept living and dining area on the main floor, where your whole group can be together. The updated, well equipped kitchen is perfect for meal preparation. We can easily host up to 6 guests, but we regret we cannot accept pets.

Good to Know

House Rules

  • 6 guests maximum
  • Check in 4:00 PM, check out 11:00 AM, no early check in or late checkout
  • Hot tub use is for guests 25 and over only
  • No pets
  • No smoking anywhere on the property
  • No parties, quiet hours are 10 PM to 8 AM
  • No fires inside or outside the cabin, wildfire risk in this area is taken very seriously
  • No alcohol or drugs at or near the hot tub
  • Please don't move the furniture
  • Start the dishwasher for any unwashed dishes before checking out
  • No refunds for events outside our control, such as weather or power outages
  • There's wildlife around the property, you may spot different animals, please respect their home

Driving & Parking

You'll pass one driveway, then a smaller one just past it that leads right to the cabin. Feel free to park anywhere along either, there's plenty of room.

  • All car types can access the cabin, a standard sedan has no trouble
  • The driveway is slightly steep, if there's snow on the ground, chains or all wheel drive are encouraged
