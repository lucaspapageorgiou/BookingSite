Tucked into the trees in Pigeon Forge, Rustic Retreat is a chalet style cabin built around a wall of windows and a soaring wood ceiling, with a spiral staircase leading up to a cozy loft. It's an easy fit for smaller groups who still want room to breathe: a private first floor primary suite, two more bedrooms and a shared bath in the loft, and a large secluded deck with a private hot tub looking out over the trees. The kitchen's green tile and stone chimney give it a warm, lived in feel, and the loft's office nook makes it easy to stay a few extra days if you're working remotely.

Good to Know

House Rules

  • 6 guests maximum
  • Check in 4:00 PM, check out 11:00 AM, no early check in or late checkout
  • Hot tub use is for guests 25 and over only
  • No pets
  • No smoking anywhere on the property
  • No parties, quiet hours are 10 PM to 8 AM

  • Please don't move the furniture
