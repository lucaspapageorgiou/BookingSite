Privacy

Privacy Policy

This page covers what information we collect on this website and how it's used. It applies to the pages you're browsing now, not to Hospitable's guest portal or the platforms (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com), which each have their own privacy policies.

Contact Form

When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, the cabin you're asking about, and your message. This information is sent to us through Formspree, our form provider, and is used only to respond to your question. We don't sell or share this information with third parties, and we don't use it for marketing.

Booking Process

If you choose to book, the reservation and payment itself are handled through Hospitable, our property management system, not through this website. Any personal or payment information you provide at that stage is subject to Hospitable's own privacy and security practices, not this site's.

Cookies & Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use it, such as which pages are viewed and where visits come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects general information like approximate location, device type, and browser. It does not collect your name, email, or payment details, and we don't use it for advertising or sell any of this information. You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt out tool.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, reach out through our Contact page.
